MEASURING STRENGTH OF TREND AND SEASONALITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Using variance-based metrics (Wang, Smith, & Hyndman, 2006):
F_T = max(0, [Var(Y - S) - Var(R)] / Var(Y))  # Trend strength
F_S = max(0, [Var(Y - T) - Var(R)] / Var(Y))  # Seasonal strength
F_T/F_S ~ 0: weak; ~ 1: strong
