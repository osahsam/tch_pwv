# Data Availability Statement

The datasets used in this study are publicly accessible from their respective repositories. The site-specific VMF3_OP products were obtained from the [TU Wien Geodesy and Geoinformation server](https://vmf.geo.tuwien.ac.at/trop_products/GNSS/VMF3/VMF3_OP/daily/) (Landskron & Böhm, 2018). The IGS zenith total delay (ZTD) products are available from the [NASA Crustal Dynamics Data Information System (CDDIS)](https://cddis.nasa.gov/archive/gnss/products/troposphere/zpd/) archive (Noll, 2010). The MERRA-2 hourly single-level meteorological data, **inst1_2d_asm_Nx (M2I1NXASM), Version 5.12.4**, used to obtain the required surface meteorological parameters are publicly available from the [NASA Goddard Earth Sciences Data and Information Services Center (GES DISC)](https://disc.gsfc.nasa.gov/datasets/M2I1NXASM_5.12.4/summary), with persistent dataset identifier [DOI: 10.5067/3Z173KIE2TPD](https://doi.org/10.5067/3Z173KIE2TPD). The ERA5-based products supplied by Wuhan University were obtained from its [public numerical weather model service](http://gmet.users.sgg.whu.edu.cn/en/customized/NWMs-based-site/submit/) (Zhang et al., 2019; Zhou et al., 2020).

The collocated 27-station PWV data, station-screening information, processed statistical outputs, manuscript figures, and MATLAB/Python analysis code supporting the findings of this study are publicly maintained in the authors' [GitHub repository](https://github.com/osahsam/tch_pwv). A versioned archival snapshot is being deposited in Zenodo.
