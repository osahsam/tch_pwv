# Data Availability Statement

The datasets used in this study are publicly accessible from their respective repositories. The site-specific VMF3_OP products were obtained from the TU Wien Geodesy and Geoinformation server (Landskron & Böhm, 2018): https://vmf.geo.tuwien.ac.at/trop_products/GNSS/VMF3/VMF3_OP/daily/. The IGS zenith total delay (ZTD) products are available from the NASA Crustal Dynamics Data Information System (CDDIS) archive (Noll, 2010): https://cddis.nasa.gov/archive/gnss/products/troposphere/zpd/. The MERRA-2 hourly single-level meteorological data, **inst1_2d_asm_Nx (M2I1NXASM), Version 5.12.4**, used to obtain the required surface meteorological parameters are publicly available from the [NASA Goddard Earth Sciences Data and Information Services Center (GES DISC)](https://disc.gsfc.nasa.gov/datasets/M2I1NXASM_5.12.4/summary), with persistent dataset identifier [DOI: 10.5067/3Z173KIE2TPD](https://doi.org/10.5067/3Z173KIE2TPD). The ERA5-based products supplied by Wuhan University were obtained from its public numerical weather model service: http://gmet.users.sgg.whu.edu.cn/en/customized/NWMs-based-site/submit/ (Zhang et al., 2019; Zhou et al., 2020).

The processed datasets, analysis outputs, figures, and computer code supporting the principal results of this study are publicly available in the authors' GitHub repository: https://github.com/osahsam/tch_pwv. The repository includes the collocated PWV data, station-screening information, 3CH/ETC/DC analysis resources, ETC-based normalised-RMSE summaries, and associated graphical outputs.
